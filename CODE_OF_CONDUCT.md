# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and implementations,
not people. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, expose private information,
or repeatedly disrupt discussions. Respect people's languages and experience.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
when needed. Enforcement should be proportional and explain the behavior involved.
For private concerns, contact [sufferfml](https://github.com/sufferfml) using a
contact method listed on the profile. Do not publish private details in an Issue.
